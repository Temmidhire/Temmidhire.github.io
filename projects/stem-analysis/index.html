Research Context

The original study used Pearson, Spearman and Kendall correlation alongside linear regression and visualized the results with scatter, box and density plots. This portfolio dashboard uses the underlying institutional CSV data to make the descriptive analysis interactive.

Source: Visualisation of Data Analysis for STEM Education Among Minorities and the accompanying LASPOTECH/LASU datasets.

Key Findings from the Research

LASPOTECH: the paper reports a Pearson correlation of 0.570063 between male and female students.
LASU: the paper reports a Pearson correlation of 0.8318226 between male and female students.
Model results: the paper reports 52.4% accuracy for the LASPOTECH regression result and 34.08% for LASU.
