Post-COVID ICT Solutions in Education & Business

A survey-based analysis of how online learning and video conferencing supported education and business during the COVID-19 disruption, with emphasis on adoption, usability, challenges and future prospects.

Descriptive Statistics Chi-Square Test RStudio 4.04 Tableau Survey Data
Project overview

From survey responses to evidence

This project examined ICT adoption after the disruption caused by COVID-19, focusing on online learning among students and video conferencing among working adults. Data was collected online from 14–17 April 2021.

Dataset & methodology

Two survey populations, one research question

Online learning survey

64 Computer Science students across HND 1–2 and ND 1–2. The study examined participation, ease of use, platform features and challenges associated with online learning.

Video conferencing survey

54 working adults. The analysis examined participation in official duties, reasons for using video conferencing, platform choice and expectations about replacing physical meetings.

Analytical workflow

1. Survey collection
Online questionnaire administered in April 2021.
2. Descriptive analysis
Response distributions and percentages were visualised using Tableau.
3. Inferential analysis
Categorical-variable relationships were tested using Pearson's chi-square test in RStudio 4.04.
4. Interpretation
Significance was assessed using a 0.05 threshold.

Research conclusion

The study examined how ICT solutions helped sustain education and business activities when physical meetings were restricted. Its findings highlight both the adoption of digital tools and the practical challenges that influence continued use, particularly around access, network/data constraints and platform usability.
